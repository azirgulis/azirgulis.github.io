Common Questions and Solutions

  • No Sound or Audio Not Playing: Make sure your device is not on silent (check the side switch on iPhone/iPad), turn the volume up, and confirm any in-app sound setting is enabled. All audio is built into the app, so no internet connection is needed for it to play.
  • Switching Languages: You can change between English and Lithuanian in the settings. Each word and sentence has its own recording in both languages.
  • Parent Settings: Options intended for grown-ups are kept in a parent area so children stay focused on learning. To open it, tap the settings icon, press and hold the lock for 3 seconds, then type the answer to a short multiplication question. Settings take effect as soon as you change them.
  • Several Children: In the parent area, under "Children", you can add a profile for each child (up to four) so each one keeps their own progress, cars and streak. The first child you add keeps the progress made so far.
  • Progress or Settings Reset: Progress and preferences are saved on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app, or resetting the device, will clear them.
  • Content Looks Cut Off or Won't Load: Try closing and reopening the app, or updating to the latest version. If it continues, email us with your device model and a screenshot.

If your question isn't answered here, email us and we'll respond as soon as possible.

Privacy Policy for Little Car Reader

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

In short: Little Car Reader collects nothing and sends nothing. Everything it saves stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and the app works without an internet connection. It is designed to be safe for children.

Little Car Reader ("we," "us," or "our") is an educational app made for young children learning to read. We take children's privacy seriously and have built the app to collect no personal data at all. This Privacy Policy explains our practices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for an email address, phone number, location, or any other contact details, and it does not require you to create an account or sign in.

If you choose to add child profiles in the parent area, the app asks for each child's name (a first name or nickname is enough) and lets you pick a car picture. These are stored only on your device, are used only to show who is playing, and are never sent to us or anyone else.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your device only:

  • its settings, such as the language, each child's age range and learning level, and accessibility and sound options;
  • learning progress: reading, handwriting practice, unlocked cars, streaks and lesson history;
  • the names and car pictures of any child profiles you create.

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting a child profile deletes that child's progress; deleting the app deletes everything. Like other app data, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example an iCloud backup), which are handled by Apple.

3. No Tracking, Advertising, or Analytics

  • No Advertising: The app contains no ads and no advertising networks.
  • No Analytics: We do not use any analytics or usage-tracking services.
  • No Tracking: We do not track you or your child across apps or websites, and we do not share data with data brokers.
  • No Third-Party Accounts: The app does not integrate social logins or third-party accounts.
  • No Network Connections: The app does not connect to the internet on its own; all words, pictures, fonts and sounds are built in.

4. Device Permissions

Little Car Reader does not request access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive device features. All sounds in the app are pre-recorded audio files included with the app and played back on the device.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for young children and is designed to comply with the principles of children's privacy laws such as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the app collects no personal information and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking, there is no personal data of a child (or anyone else) for us to access, use, or disclose.

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not rely on third-party services to collect or process personal data. The only link out of the app is an optional "Rate on the App Store" button inside the parent area, behind the parental gate. It opens Apple's App Store, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing.

7. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to lose or expose. Any information saved by the app stays within your device's own protected storage.

8. Changes to This Policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Last Updated" date above.
