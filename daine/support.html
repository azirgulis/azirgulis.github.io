Halloween Monster Bus: Support

Halloween Monster Bus is a set of illustrated, branching spooky stories for children aged 6 to 10. Ride Thirteen, the Monster Bus, and its crew; every page has a picture, the text on screen, and a "Read to me" button.

App: Halloween Monster Bus

Bundle ID: org.kastalia.daine

Platform: iPhone and iPad

Contact: arasmcz@gmail.com

FAQ

How do the stories unlock?

The first story is always open. Each next stop on the route opens once the story before it reaches a good or golden ending. Spooky endings never lock you out: choose "Go back and choose again" and try another way.

How do I turn reading aloud or sound effects on or off?

Tap Me at the bottom of the route. Under Reading you can choose "Read aloud automatically" and "Sound effects".

How do I start over?

Progress is saved only on the device. A grown-up can clear it under Me → For grown-ups → Clear all progress. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The stories, pictures and narration are all in the app. Only sending feedback needs the internet.

Does the app collect any data?

No ads, no accounts, no analytics or tracking, and no personal information. The only thing that ever leaves the device is an optional written message a grown-up chooses to send us. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what it contains.

I found a typo or a bug. What should I do?

A grown-up can use Me → For grown-ups → Send feedback in the app, or email us at arasmcz@gmail.com.
