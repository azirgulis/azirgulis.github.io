Privacy Policy for Halloween Monster Bus

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Halloween Monster Bus is a story app for children aged 6 to 10: illustrated, branching spooky stories with a "Read to me" option. We built it so that children can play without anyone collecting information about them.

The short version

  • No ads.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not collect personal information.
  • Story progress stays on the device.

What stays on the device

The stories, pictures and narration are part of the app and work offline. Progress (endings found, badges, streaks, the daily ride) and settings (read aloud automatically, sound effects) are saved only on the device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them, and a grown-up can also clear them in the app under Me → For grown-ups → Clear all progress.

Feedback from grown-ups (optional)

Behind a "grown-ups only" check, the app has a form for sending us a written message. Nothing is sent unless a grown-up writes a message and taps Send feedback. A message is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and contains only:

  • the message text, the topic and any tags chosen for it, and which screen of the app it was sent from;
  • the date and time it was sent;
  • the app version and the device's operating system and its version (for example, iOS 18).

It does not include a name, email address, account or any device identifier, and the form asks grown-ups not to write personal details. Like any internet connection, sending a message reveals the network address it comes from to Google's servers; it is not saved with the message. We use messages only to fix problems and improve the stories. Google processes this data for us under the Firebase privacy terms.

Sharing the app

"Share the app" (also behind the grown-ups check) opens the device's own share sheet with a link to the App Store. We don't see who it is shared with.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a feedback message contains a child's personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Earlier versions (Story Nexus)

Before version 2.0 this app was called Story Nexus. Story Nexus 1.x used Google Firebase Analytics to count how the app was used, and its feedback form had an optional email field. Version 2.0 removed both. To have an old feedback message deleted, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
