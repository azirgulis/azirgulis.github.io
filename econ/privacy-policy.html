Privacy Policy for Econ App

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Econ App ("we," "us," or "our") is an educational tool designed to provide economics education services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices for the Econ App, emphasizing limited data collection and user control.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our educational services:

Personal Information

  • Email Addresses: Collected for authentication via Firebase Authentication's email link system, only if you choose to create an account.
  • User Onboarding Preferences: Information such as user type (e.g., student, professional) and learning goals, stored in Firebase Firestore for personalized learning experiences.
  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous device IDs generated for users who access core features without signing in.

Usage Data

  • Analytics Data: Screen views, user interactions, and onboarding completion tracked via Firebase Analytics to improve app functionality.
  • AI Usage Tracking: Daily query counts and usage patterns for the AI tutor, stored in Firestore and linked to user accounts (if applicable).
  • AI Tutor Questions: Questions you send to the AI tutor are saved with your account in Firestore. From the autumn 2026 update on, the app asks for your permission before it sends anything to the AI, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy → “AI tutor”.
  • Reports of AI Content: If you report an AI answer as offensive, wrong or otherwise problematic, we store the report so we can review it: the answer (shortened), the reason, any note you add, the lesson, the app version, your account ID and the time. Reports are kept for moderation even if you later delete your account.
  • Learning Progress: Lesson completion, scores, and progress stored locally on your device and optionally synced to Firestore for cloud backup.
  • App Behavior Data: Button clicks, navigation patterns, and feature usage to enhance user experience.

Technical Data

  • Voice Input: If you dictate a question to the AI tutor, your phone’s built-in speech recognition (from Apple or Google) turns it into text. We never receive or store the audio.
  • Local Storage: User preferences, progress, and settings cached locally on your device for offline access.
  • Network Data: Internet connection required for Firebase services and AI tutoring features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and enhance the educational features of the Econ App, including:

  • Delivering personalized economics lessons and AI tutoring.
  • Tracking learning progress to support your educational goals.
  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve app performance and user experience.
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Econ App:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database storage (Firestore), analytics, and AI-related services.
  • Google Vertex AI: Powers the AI tutor with Google’s Gemini models (current versions of the app go through our own Cloud Function; versions up to 2.2.3 call Vertex AI directly). To answer, it receives your question, the earlier messages in that chat and the lesson page you are on. Under Google Cloud’s terms, Google does not use this content to train its models.
  • Speech-to-Text: Your phone’s built-in speech recognition service (Apple or Google) for voice input; its use is governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy, and we do not receive the audio.

These services are bound by strict confidentiality agreements, and we do not share your personal information beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

4. Key Privacy Commitments

We prioritize your privacy with the following practices:

  • No Audio Storage: We never receive or store voice recordings.
  • Anonymous Usage Allowed: You can use core app features without creating an account, relying on anonymous device IDs.
  • Limited AI Usage: AI tutor questions are limited per day to ensure fair usage.
  • Local-First Approach: Key data, such as learning progress and preferences, is stored locally, with optional cloud backup via Firestore.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The Econ App does not use advertising networks or share data for advertising purposes.
  • Educational Purpose: All data is used solely to provide economics education services.

5. Permissions Explained

The Econ App requires the following permissions:

  • Internet/Network: Necessary for Firebase services and AI tutoring features.
  • Microphone/Audio: Optional, used for voice input to the AI tutor.
  • Bluetooth: Optional, used for speech recognition functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption for data stored in Firebase. However, no system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your device secure.

7. Your Choices and Control

You have control over your data:

  • You can choose to delete your account in the app at anytime. All of your data will be permanently erased.
  • Use the app anonymously without creating an account.
  • Disable optional features like voice input to avoid microphone usage.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you via the app or our website, and the updated policy will be posted at https://azirgulis.github.io/econ/privacy-policy.html.
