# Ekonomika ir Verslumas

A mobile app helping Lithuanian gymnasium students (grades 11–12) prepare for the **Ekonomika ir verslumas** state matura exam. It is an educational product of **ISM University of Management and Economics**, distributed free of charge, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

This page is the official support hub for the app. If you found a bug, have a question, or want to delete your data — the information is below.

## What the app offers

- **13 topics** covering the official Ekonomika ir verslumas curriculum (from demand and supply to personal finance)
- **Real past matura exams** (2025 main and retake sessions) with detailed result analysis
- **Mock exam** and per-topic unit tests
- **Formula reference** and **notation glossary** with explanations
- **Step-by-step problem solver**
- **Spaced repetition** review system
- **Daily streaks** to build a learning habit
- **Interactive charts** (Phillips curve, GDP comparisons, cost functions, etc.)
- **Lithuanian and English** UI
- **Dark mode**

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the app really free?
Yes. No in-app purchases, no ads, no subscription. This is a gift from ISM University to Lithuanian high school students.

### Do I need to register?
No. You can use the app without an account. Your progress is stored on your device.

### How do I switch between Lithuanian and English?
Open **Settings → Language** and pick the language you want. The change takes effect immediately.

### How do I enable dark mode?
**Settings → Appearance → Theme**. You can choose light, dark, or follow the system setting.

### I lost my streak — can I recover it?
A streak breaks if you skip a day. Unfortunately we cannot restore it, but a new streak starts as soon as you complete at least one lesson again.

### My progress is gone or a lesson won't open
First try:
1. Close the app and reopen it
2. Check your internet connection
3. Make sure you have the latest version (App Store / Google Play)

If the issue persists, email [aras.zirgulis@ism.lt](mailto:aras.zirgulis@ism.lt) with your device model, OS version, and a short description.

### I found an error in a formula or lesson
Thank you. Please email [aras.zirgulis@ism.lt](mailto:aras.zirgulis@ism.lt) with:
- Topic name and lesson number
- What you think should be corrected
- A brief explanation or source (if available)

### Does the app follow the official VBE curriculum?
Yes. Content is aligned with the Ekonomika ir verslumas state matura exam program approved by the National Education Agency. Real past exam variants are included.

### Which devices are supported?
- **iOS:** 13.0 or newer
- **Android:** 7.0 (Nougat) or newer

### How do I delete my data?
See the dedicated page → [Data Deletion](./data-deletion.html). It explains the full procedure under GDPR Article 17.
